ThemeCC has no accounts, no comments, no ads, and no payments. We do not ask you for personal information.

## Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and how often install commands are copied. We use it to see which pages and themes are useful.

- Analytics storage is **denied by default**. Google Analytics sets cookies only after you choose **Accept** in the banner.
- If you choose **Decline**, the Google Analytics script still loads, but it is told not to store anything on your device. Google may still receive limited, cookieless signals such as the page address and your approximate location from your IP address.
- Your choice is stored in your browser's local storage under the key `themecc-consent`. Clearing site data asks you again.
- The events we record are: install and marketplace command copies, preview plays, outbound clicks to a theme's repository, submit clicks, and filter use. They contain the theme name and type, not anything about you.

Preview deployments of the site and local builds do not load analytics.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Vercel keeps standard server logs, such as IP addresses and request times, as described in the Vercel privacy policy.

## Links to other sites

Theme pages link to GitHub repositories run by theme authors. We do not control those sites. Their own policies apply once you leave ThemeCC.

## Contact and removal

Open an issue in the [ThemeCC repository](https://github.com/LinXiaoTao/themecc/issues) with questions or requests about this policy.
